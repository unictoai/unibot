Why unibot is different.

01

Privacy-first, for real

Your data never leaves your phone. A local-only kill-switch blocks all non-essential network, and the network traffic log shows you every connection. No analytics, no ads, no trackers — provably zero.

02

Bring your own key

No account, no subscription, no vendor lock-in. Paste an API key from any provider — OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek — stored encrypted on your device.

03

Fully open source

GPL-3.0. Every line auditable, every claim verifiable. Fork it, inspect it, build it yourself — trust through transparency.

04

Offline AI

Download open models and chat in airplane mode. Your conversations with on-device models never touch the internet at all.
