unibot is the private, open-source AI agent for Android. No account, no subscription, zero trackers — free forever under GPL-3.0.

YOUR KEY, YOUR RULES
Bring your own API key from any provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek and more). The app talks directly to the provider you choose — keys stay encrypted on your phone and never leave it. No middleman server, no relay.

OR GO FULLY OFFLINE
Download a language model and chat with zero network — airplane mode works. Your words never leave your pocket.

31 CONNECTORS
Gmail, Drive, Calendar, GitHub, Telegram, YouTube, Discord, Spotify, Notion, Reddit, WhatsApp, Trello, Todoist, GitLab, Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Tasks, weather, currency, Wikipedia, Hacker News, dictionary, translate, Stack Overflow, TMDB, news, QR tools, RSS, Photos, Outlook.

AN AGENT, NOT A CHATBOT
Web search built into chat with tappable sources. Encrypted long-term memory. Scheduled agents and morning briefings. Creator studio with YouTube dashboard, title optimizer, captions and hashtags.

PRIVACY YOU CAN VERIFY
Privacy dashboard, local-only kill-switch, and a network traffic log showing every connection the app makes. Chat lock, auto-delete, auto-clear clipboard, permission audit. Don't trust the claims — check them.
